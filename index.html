Research

Evidence for a question every AI product faces: when do people trust a recommendation?

MSc Dissertation — Certainty and Justification Framing in Music Recommendation (Distinction)

Northumbria University, Newcastle, UK / 2025 - 2026

Tested whether the way a recommendation is framed changes whether people trust it. I ran a 2×2 between-subjects experiment (certainty × justification type) using 60 tracks across 14 genres, set the sample size in advance with a simulation-based power analysis, and modelled 118 responses with linear mixed-effects models in R. Justification raised trust (p = .009) and willingness to explore (p = .002), but only for listeners already familiar with the music. The product takeaway: explanations don't help every user equally, so they should be tested per audience, not assumed.

Discovery Gap — Does Streaming Discovery Fail Smaller Artists?

Product Analysis  ·  Music Streaming  ·  A data-led product write-up that ends in a feature proposal.

1.44M plays · 10 listeners

Discovery Gap

Independent Product Case Study  ·  Python  ·  SQL / MySQL

I analysed 1.44M plays from 10 listeners' Spotify histories against Last.fm data to test whether discovery works worse for smaller artists. It does: only 12% of lesser-known artists were still being played 30–90 days after discovery, against 19% of big artists, and all 10 listeners showed the gap. The write-up turns that into a product proposal, "Second Listen", a feature to give a liked first listen of a smaller artist a second chance. All 10 listeners consented to their data being published.

MOV.IE REC

Product & Engineering, Solo  ·  Live Web App  ·  Python / Flask

A live movie and TV recommender I took from idea to public launch on my own. Recommendations come from a two-stage pipeline: TF-IDF content matching finds candidates, then reranking for quality, recency and collaborative filtering orders them. It covers 20+ genres, 5 languages and 22+ regional markets, with Google sign-in, a personal watchlist and live TMDB data. The biggest constraint was size: I cut the similarity index by 97% (1.24 GB to 30 MB) so it could run on Render with PostgreSQL.

Cuepoint — Open-Source DJ Tool

Built & Published  ·  Music Tech  ·  Python  ·  Tells a DJ what to play next and the exact bar to bring it in. 1,700+ downloads on PyPI.

pip install cuepoint · 1,700+ downloads

Cuepoint

Product & Engineering, Solo  ·  Open Source  ·  Python, NumPy, SciPy

DJ tools say two records match because they share a key and tempo, then the mix falls apart live. Cuepoint reads an already-analysed rekordbox library (read-only), recommends the next track and the exact mix-in point, and plays the transition back so the DJ can hear the call instead of trusting it. Each suggestion is scored across 7 weighted components, with thresholds measured from real audio rather than guessed. Published as open source on PyPI, where it has 1,700+ downloads.
